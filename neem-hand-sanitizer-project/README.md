# Herbal Neem Hand Sanitizer — Chemistry Laboratory Project

A modern, responsive, and scientific presentation website for our Chemistry Laboratory Practical: **"Preparation of Herbal Hand Sanitizer using WHO Formulation with Natural Neem Leaf (Azadirachta indica) Extract"**.

---

## 🧪 Project Information & Stoichiometry

| Ingredient | Chemical Formula | Stock Conc. | Volume (for 100 mL Batch) | Volume % | Scientific Function |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Ethanol (Rectified Spirit)** | $C_2H_5OH$ | 95.0% v/v | **83.14 mL** *(or 83.3 mL)* | 83.14% | Primary antimicrobial agent; disrupts lipid envelopes & denatures microbial proteins |
| **Hydrogen Peroxide** | $H_2O_2$ | 3.0% v/v | **4.17 mL** | 4.17% | Destroys bacterial endospores in solution & containers (sporicidal preservation) |
| **Glycerol (Glycerin)** | $C_3H_8O_3$ | 98.0% v/v | **1.47 mL** *(or 1.45 mL)* | 1.47% | Emollient & humectant; binds water to skin to prevent alcohol-induced dryness |
| **Neem Leaf Extract ("Nim Pan")** | Herbal Phyto | Vatting decoction | **5.00 – 8.00 mL** | ~6.00% | Natural antibacterial bioactives (Azadirachtin, Nimbin), anti-inflammatory & botanical fragrance |
| **Sterile Distilled Water** | $H_2O$ | Pure / Sterile | **q.s. to 100.00 mL** | Balance (~5.22%) | Solvent carrier and volume adjustment to 100 mL graduation mark |

## 🌟 Key Features of the Website

1. **Responsive & Modern Design**:
   - Clean medical-botanical emerald aesthetic with glassmorphic cards, Lucide icons, and Tailwind styling.
   - Fully optimized for desktop, laptops, tablets, mobile phones, and classroom projectors.

2. **Interactive Live Batch Calculator**:
   - Calculate any custom batch size (e.g. 50 mL, 100 mL, 250 mL, 500 mL, 1000 mL).
   - Real-time dynamic recalculation with individual ingredient progress bars and volumetric sum verification.
   - **One-click "Copy Recipe"** button to copy calculated values directly to your clipboard.

3. **Digitalized Laboratory Flowchart & Timeline**:
   - Step 1: Workstation Sterilization & Disinfection
   - Step 2: Fresh Neem Leaf Vatting & Extraction
   - Step 3: Precision Gravimetric / Volumetric Measurement
   - Step 4: Stepwise Chemical Homogenization (Ethanol + $H_2O_2$ + Glycerol)
   - Step 5: Infusion of Neem Extract & Volume Make-Up to 100 mL
   - Step 6: Transfer to Clean Dispenser, Labeling & 72-Hour Spore Quarantine

4. **Real Laboratory Photo Gallery with Zoom Lightbox**:
   - Displays all 5 authentic lab photos:
     - Neem leaf boiling on Bunsen burner with wire gauze
     - Team member actively monitoring and stirring the decoction
     - 3-member team measuring liquid in graduated cylinder
     - Final hand sanitizer mixture in graduated beaker
     - Handwritten laboratory notebook flowchart record
   - Click on any image to open full-screen high-resolution lightbox view.

5. **Safety & Quality Control (QC)**:
   - Flammability precautions (Class 1B Flammable liquid).
   - $H_2O_2$ oxidizer safety and nitrile gloves PPE recommendations.
   - WHO 72-hour quarantine rationale.
   - QC metrics: pH 6.2–6.8, optical clarity, evaporation time (15–25s), and alcohol strength (>78% v/v).

6. **Interactive Chemistry Quiz**:
   - 3 interactive questions with instant color-coded feedback to test and demonstrate conceptual mastery.

7. **3-Member Student Team & Mentor Section**:
   - Dedicated cards for all 3 group members.
   - Inline editable fields (click to type your actual names, roll numbers, and division).
   - Special acknowledgment card for your respected Chemistry Faculty ("Ma'am").

8. **One-Click Academic Report Printing**:
   - Built-in print stylesheet: Click **"Print Report"** to generate a clean, formatted report ready for submission or PDF export.
