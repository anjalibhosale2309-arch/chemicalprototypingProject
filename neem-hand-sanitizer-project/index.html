Chemical Composition for 100 mL Batch

Every component in this sanitizer formulation serves a distinct scientific function, from direct pathogen deactivation to emollient protection and anti-microbial synergy.

C₂H₅OH

Ethanol (95%)

Primary Active Antiseptic

Quantity (100ml): 83.14 mL
Notebook Ref: 83.3 mL
Volume %: ~80% final alc.

Role: Rapidly denatures microbial proteins and dissolves lipid membranes of bacteria and enveloped viruses (e.g. coronaviruses, influenza).

H₂O₂

Hydrogen Peroxide (3%)

Sporicidal Agent

Quantity (100ml): 4.17 mL
Stock Conc.: 3.0% v/v
Final Conc.: ~0.125%

Role: Does NOT target skin pathogens; it inactivates contaminating bacterial endospores present in bulk solution or containers, ensuring sterile shelf life.

C₃H₈O₃

Glycerol (98%)

Humectant & Emollient

Quantity (100ml): 1.47 mL
Notebook Ref: 1.45 mL
Texture Effect: Smooth / Moist

Role: High alcohol concentrations cause severe epidermal xerosis (drying/cracking). Glycerol binds water to the stratum corneum, keeping skin supple and hydrated.

A. indica

Neem Extract + H₂O

Herbal Bioactive Carrier

Quantity (100ml): 11.22 mL
Extraction: Aqueous Boil
Total Volume: q.s. 100 mL

Role: Pure distilled water brings volume to mark. Neem leaves ("nim pan") infuse Azadirachtin, triterpenoids, and flavonoids for natural antiseptic aroma and antibacterial boost.

The Science of "Nim Pan" (Neem Leaves) Extract

While standard chemical sanitizers rely entirely on synthetic chemicals, our project incorporates an aqueous decoction of freshly boiled neem leaves (popularly referred to as "nim pan vatting mixture").

Azadirachtin Bioactive

Potent triterpenoid possessing recognized antibacterial, antifungal, and antiviral barrier actions.

Natural Herbal Aroma

Eliminates the sharp, harsh chemical odor of industrial alcohol, replacing it with refreshing botanical freshness.

Skin Soothing

Rich in nimbidin and quercetin flavonoids that calm irritated skin and counteract ethanol erythema.

Vatting Extraction Process Observed in Lab:

  1. Leaf Selection: Fresh green mature neem leaves harvested, washed thoroughly with deionized water to remove surface particulates.
  2. Thermal Maceration / Boiling: Leaves placed in a 250 mL Borosil beaker with distilled water, heated over a wire gauze using a burner (see lab photos).
  3. Phytochemical Leaching: Simmered until the solution turned rich olive-green, concentrating water-soluble tannins and limonoids.
  4. Filtration: Cooled and filtered through qualitative filter paper to yield a clear, particulate-free aqueous extract.

Notebook Observation Note:

Title: Food Additive / Hand Sanitizer Flow Chart

Quantities noted: Ethanol 83.3 mL, H₂O₂ 4.17 mL, Glycerol 1.45 mL, Water up to 100 mL.

01

Clean & Disinfect Workspace

Preparation

Wipe all laboratory benches, measuring cylinders, beakers, and glass stirrers with 70% alcohol to eliminate airborne dust and cross-contaminants before handling reagents.

02

Neem Leaf Vatting & Extraction

Phyto Step

Weigh freshly washed neem leaves ("nim pan"). Submerge in deionized water within a 250 mL beaker on wire gauze. Heat using a burner while stirring continuously until active polyphenols and azadirachtin leach out. Filter and cool to room temperature.

03

Precision Volumetric Measurement

Measurement

Using a clean graduated measuring cylinder:
• Measure 83.14 mL of 95% Ethanol.
• Measure 4.17 mL of 3% Hydrogen Peroxide ($H_2O_2$).
• Measure 1.47 mL of 98% Glycerol using a precision pipette or graduated syringe.

04

Stepwise Chemical Homogenization

Mixing

Pour the ethanol into the primary mixing vessel. Add $H_2O_2$ and stir. Next, add Glycerol — because glycerol is dense and viscous, rinse the measuring cylinder with a portion of distilled water to ensure full transfer, then stir thoroughly.

05

Neem Infusion & Volume Make-Up

Final Volume

Add the prepared neem extract. Add sterile distilled water slowly until the meniscus reaches exactly the 100 mL graduation mark. Stir gently for 2 minutes to achieve complete homogenization.

06

Bottling, Labeling & 72-Hour Quarantine

Storage & QC

Immediately decant the solution into clean, airtight bottles. Affix a chemical label (Batch date, ingredients, flammability warning). Store unopened for 72 hours prior to use to permit $H_2O_2$ to destroy any spore contaminants.

Laboratory Safety & Quality Assurance

Safety Protocols & Chemical Hazard Control

Alcohol rubs contain flammable solvents and oxidizing agents requiring stringent laboratory handling protocols.

High Flammability (Flash Point ~17.5°C)

Ethanol vapours ignite readily. Keep containers tightly closed. Never expose bulk alcohol to open flames, electrical hotplates without spark protection, or smoking zones.

Hydrogen Peroxide Safety

$H_2O_2$ is a corrosive oxidizer. Avoid direct contact with eyes or mucous membranes. Wear protective nitrile gloves and safety goggles during volumetric dispensing.

Mandatory 72-Hour Quarantine

Per WHO guidelines, sealed sanitizer bottles must rest for at least 72 hours before consumer use to give $H_2O_2$ sufficient contact time to destroy any spores from raw containers.
